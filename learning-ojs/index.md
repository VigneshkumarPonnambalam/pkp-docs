GUIDELINES
Article uniqueness should be above 85%
Title: not too long below 20 words, Times New Roman 14-point, boldface type, centered. Capitalize the first letter of nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs; do not capitalize articles, coordinate conjunctions, or prepositions.
File format should in word (doc, docx)
Abstract should not exceed 250 words with keywords (4 to 6)
Page Layout: 8.5 x 11-inch paper; for A4 paper
Use Times News Roman font
Follow the Title Case in all the section level headings
First-level headings: Times New Roman 13-point boldface, Title Case, Justify, Top and Bottom: 6pt.
Second-level headings: Times New Roman 12-point boldface, Title Case, Justify, Top and Bottom: 6pt.
Third-level headings: Times New Roman 11-point boldface, Title Case, Justify, Top and Bottom: 6pt.
Reference: Use Times New Roman 10-point type.
Acknowledgements should be brief and placed at the end of the text before the references.
All paragraphs should be indented 1 pica (approximately 0.17-inch or 0.422 cm).
Author names and affiliations are to be centered beneath the title and printed in Times New Roman 12-point, non-boldface type. Multiple authors may be shown in a two or three-column format, with their affiliations below their respective names. Affiliations are centered below each author name, italicized, not bold. Include e-mail addresses.
Same Author should not be presented more than 3 articles in a same Issue.
Don't use enter key in the article title. Please Mention the title in single or double line
Mention the Author names and Affiliations as per the given Format below, Author 1, Author 2, Author 3..etc Affiliation 1 Affiliation 2 Affiliation 3 . . etc (All Affiliations should be in the format of "Department, University/Organization, place, country").
As per Journal standard Roles/Degree are not allowed in Author group and affiliations. Kindly remove the roles/degree in author group and affiliations.
Figures and Tables used in the manuscript should be in sequential order with respective captions and citations. (Individual caption should be present for all figures/tables)
Update all figures as whole images instead of grouping multiple elements together.
Figures should not be Fig 1a, 1b, 1c..etc. Include all the part labels (a, b, c...etc) inside the respective images and ensure all the figures are whole/single images like Figure 1, Figure 2, Figure 3..etc with individual captions as “Figure 1. Caption content; Figure 2. Caption Content…etc)
Kindly avoid using Enter key in tables. Create an individual rows and columns for every line.
Tables should be in Editable format instead of image format. (By using Insert Table/Table design Tab you can modify)
Mention all the formulas in equation format by using insert-Equation option. (Note: Please note that you can frame the equation as equation mode in word doc. by using Insert equation option (i.e., In word doc., Near by Home, Insert option will be there, After You click the Insert option You can see the symbol phi at last, in which you can edit your equation by clicking the insert new equation option.)
Both reference citations as well as references in reference list should be in Numbered format as like [1], [2], [3],..etc.
Need to mention at least 20 References.
Reference part should be update as per the Author Guideline.
Author name, initial, year, title of the paper, Journal name, Volume/issue, page number, Link “For example: [For an article in a journal] [1] Goss, R. O., 1990, Economic policies and seaports: Strategies for port authorities. Maritime Policy and Management, 17(4), 273-287, https://www.texilajournal.com/
Minimum word count of the article should be above 3000 words.
All the sections - i.e., “Methods, Results, Discussion” should be with statistical data.
Conflict of interest, Ethical Approval should be present in the manuscript.
